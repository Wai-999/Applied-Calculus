# Burmese localization notes: chapters 4–6

These are original Burmese translations of the platform's original English companion lessons, not copied textbook translations and not a claim of certification by a human language reviewer.

## Research basis

- [Unicode Consortium: Myanmar FAQ](https://unicode.org/faq/myanmar.html) distinguishes Unicode Myanmar from incompatible legacy encodings and explains combining-character order and Unicode-compatible fonts. The assets use Unicode Burmese, with ordinary prose spacing; formulas keep their original notation.
- [Yadanabon University, Department of Mathematics](https://ydnbu.edu.mm/course/department-of-mathematics/) lists English course titles including Differential and Integral Calculus and Calculus of Several Variables. This supports keeping recognizable English technical anchors in a Burmese learning aid. The page contains unrelated law-module descriptions, so those descriptions were not used as mathematical evidence or a Burmese glossary.
- The [2024 Grade 12 Mathematics textbook published by the NUG Ministry of Education](https://edu4mm.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/G-12-Maths-Textbook-May-13-2024.pdf) was located in search, but its full PDF could not be inspected through the browsing tool because of its size. It is therefore not claimed as verification of any Burmese wording.

## Editorial decisions

- Follow the project's agreed terms: ဆင်းသက်ကိန်း (derivative), အင်တီဂရယ် (integral), ဖန်ရှင် (function), ကိန်းရှင် (variable), သတ်မှတ်နယ်ပယ် (domain), and တိမ်းစောင်းနှုန်း (slope).
- Explain မူလဖန်ရှင် (antiderivative) as a function whose derivative returns the stated rate. These descriptive Burmese terms are not presented as the only officially prescribed translations.
- Distinguish a definite integral by its stated endpoint bounds; distinguish signed net accumulation from nonnegative geometric area and total movement.
- Use Burmese descriptions together with English anchors for L’Hôpital, Riemann, Lorenz, Gini, Simpson, trapezoidal rule, consumer surplus and producer surplus.
- Keep amounts, dollars, times, equations, answers, tolerance thresholds, choice order and scientific units such as m² intact. Currency is not converted into kyat.
- The translations retain all source explanation paragraphs, examples, conditions, hints, numerical solution reasoning, misconception notes, transfer prompts and fictional prediction scenarios. The @ character-name placeholder remains intact.

## Validation

The module imports successfully. All 15 requested IDs are present; lesson-step, question, symbol, derivation, worked-example, concept-check and mission arrays align with their English sources. Every prose field contains Burmese text; notation-only scientific units remain unchanged. The parent integration checks formulas, numerical answers and selection indices independently.
