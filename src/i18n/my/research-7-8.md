# Burmese content and terminology research: chapters 7–8

Research performed 4 November 2026. This document records sources and editorial choices; it is not a claim of an official or certified Burmese glossary.

## Primary sources consulted

- [Unicode Consortium, Myanmar FAQ](https://www.unicode.org/faq/myanmar.html): character encoding, Unicode-compatible font families, and the distinction between Unicode and legacy Zawgyi encodings.
- [Unicode Standard, Myanmar script](https://www.unicode.org/versions/Unicode18.0.0/core-spec/chapter-16/): combining sequences and Myanmar spacing conventions. Text is stored as actual Unicode, with natural phrase spaces; rendering must preserve script clusters.
- [Unicode Technical Note 11, Representing Myanmar in Unicode](https://www.unicode.org/notes/tn11/myanmar_uni-v2.pdf): line-breaking considerations for Myanmar phrases. Its guidance informs typography, rather than supplying mathematical terminology.
- [Mandalar University, Mathematics curriculum](https://www.mdl.edu.mm/mathematics-subject/): the local curriculum names calculus, differential equations, statistics, and multivariable courses using English technical names.
- [Pathein University, Department of Mathematics](https://www.pathein-u.edu.mm/department-of-mathematics/): local course descriptions include partial derivatives, gradients and differential equations.
- [University of Information Technology, Calculus I](https://uit.edu.mm/degree-programs/cst-11201-syllabus/): an applied university syllabus using the English terms derivative, related rates, and maxima/minima.
- [OpenStax, Defining the Derivative](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/3-1-defining-the-derivative): a primary textbook reference for the distinction between average and instantaneous change.
- [OpenStax, Partial Derivatives](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-3/pages/4-3-partial-derivatives): primary textbook reference for held-fixed input conditions.
- [OpenStax, The Regression Equation](https://openstax.org/books/introductory-statistics-2e/pages/12-3-the-regression-equation): checked residual sign and squared-error conventions.

No textbook passages or exercises from these web sources were copied. The lesson translations follow this repository’s original companion content. The terminology definitions, usage notes and numerical examples are authored specifically for the learning platform.

## Editorial decisions

- The catalog is scoped to this book and platform, not to all of mathematics.
- Use readable Burmese explanations with English technical anchors. Local primary university sources use many English names; they do not establish a single official Burmese translation for every specialist term.
- Shared vocabulary: ဖန်ရှင် (function), ကိန်းရှင် (variable), သတ်မှတ်နယ်ပယ် (domain), တိမ်းစောင်းနှုန်း (slope), လင်မစ် (limit), ဆင်းသက်ကိန်း (derivative), လော့ဂရစ်သမ် (logarithm), အင်တီဂရယ် (integral). The derivative entry also includes ဒေရီဗေးတစ် as a searchable pronunciation alias.
- Explain partial derivatives by the input held fixed; explain differentials as local first-order estimates rather than exact changes.
- Distinguish a level curve’s fixed output from a cross section’s fixed input.
- State the regularity condition for Lagrange multipliers and preserve separate boundary/feasibility checks. A multiplier equation yields candidates, not automatic global optima.
- Keep the difference between association and causation explicit. Residual means observed minus predicted; squared errors retain output-squared units.
- Preserve all original mathematical symbols, numerical values, currency assumptions and answer metadata. Do not convert dollars to kyat, because that would change the models.
- Keep mathematical examples in international digits for consistent notation. Language choice should not change answers, mastery or lesson IDs.
- English and Burmese examples describe the same calculations and assumptions.
